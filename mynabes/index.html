MyNabes.

Neighborhood app

MyNabes is a community app designed to bring neighbors closer and make everyday connections more meaningful.

  • Illustration
  • Animation
  • Communication assets
Watch the video →

THE PROJECT

A stronger
neighborhood.
Together.

I developed the visual direction, creating a friendly, colorful and inclusive visual language that makes the idea of community feel approachable and engaging.

Share

Share items, help each other and support your neighbors.

Connect

Connect with people who live near you and build real relationships.

Stay informed

Get local updates, announcements and important alerts.

Get help

Ask for help or offer your support to those who need it.

THE CHALLENGE

Create engagement from day one.

As a new app in a competitive space, My Nabes needed to quickly communicate trust, warmth and community, while standing out across multiple platforms.

THE IDEA

Turning community into
a visual language.

The goal was to make MyNabes feel less like a technology platform and more like a place where people belong. I created a colorful illustration style, friendly characters that work consistently across the app, social media and communications.
